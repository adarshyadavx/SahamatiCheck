Dosa Notes Privacy Policy

This is a fabricated privacy policy for a fictional demo app, Dosa Notes.

Last updated: 15 September 2026.

What we collect

Dosa Notes collects your email address to create and sync your account.

We store the photos you attach to notes so that they can sync across your devices.

We collect crash logs and device identifiers to fix bugs.

We do not collect your location.

We do not collect your contacts.

Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except with service providers that host our servers.

We never sell your personal data.

Your choices

You can delete your account and notes at any time from the settings screen.
